# 📊 Maven Market – Power BI Sales Dashboard

## 🛒 Project Overview
A Power BI analytics dashboard built for **Maven Market**, a multi-national grocery retailer operating in the **United States, Canada, and Mexico**.  
The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance and identify growth opportunities across different markets.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Track **Revenue, Profit, Margin %, and Return Rate**
- Compare performance across **countries, regions, and stores**
- Analyze **time-based trends** with drill-downs by product, customer, and store
- Identify **operational issues** and **growth opportunities**

---

## 🗂 Dataset
The dataset consists of multiple retail-related tables:
- Sales / Transactions  
- Returns  
- Products  
- Customers  
- Stores  
- Regions  
- Calendar  

---

## 📈 Key KPIs
- Total Revenue  
- Total Profit  
- Profit Margin %  
- Quantity Sold & Returned  
- Return Rate %  

---

## 📄 Report Pages
- **Executive Overview:** KPIs, sales trends, and geographic performance  
- **Products:** Brand and category analysis with product-level margins  
- **Customers:** Top customers and basic segmentation  
- **Returns:** Return trends and high-return hotspots  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Strong overall performance with a high profit margin (~59.67%)  
- Monthly revenue shows a gradual decline over time  
- The **United States** is the primary driver of revenue and profit  
- **Canada and Mexico** present clear growth opportunities  
- Returns are low overall but concentrated in specific products
